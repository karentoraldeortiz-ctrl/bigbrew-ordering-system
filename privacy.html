To ensure accuracy, this Privacy Notice is updated periodically. This notice was last updated on April 2026.

Last Updated: April 2026

At BigBrew Maysan, we value your privacy. This document explains what information we collect and how we use it for your online pre-orders.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect the following:

  • Name: To identify your order at the pick-up point.
  • Email: To notify you regarding your order status/
  • Order Details: Your chosen drinks and add-ons.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for:

  • Processing your pre-order.
  • Communicating with you if an item is out of stock.
  • Improving our shop's services.

3. Data Protection

We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party companies. Your data is used exclusively for this transaction.
