About Us

Big Brew Maysan is a local milktea shop that offers a wide variety of delicious and refreshing milktea flavors. We are committed to providing our customers with high-quality beverages made from the finest ingredients. Our mission is to create a welcoming and enjoyable experience for all our customers, whether they are looking for a quick pick-me-up or a place to relax and socialize. At Big Brew Maysan, we believe that great taste should be accessible to everyone, which is why we strive to offer our products at affordable prices. We are passionate about what we do and are dedicated to bringing joy and satisfaction to every cup we serve.
